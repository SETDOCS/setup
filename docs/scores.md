# How you are scored

**You are ranked on your Sharpe ratio, not your profit.**

<hr>

## When your score appears
A Sharpe needs at least three days of returns, so first scores show up around 26 November, at roughly the same time for everyone, because the window starts everybody together.
<br><br>
You are ranked from your first scored day. There is no waiting period and no probation. The Days column shows how much history your score rests on, so anyone reading the board can see the difference between a four-day score and a forty-day one.
<br><br>
The board updates about once an hour.

<hr>

## The leaderboard

| Column | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **#** | Your rank. A dash means there is no Sharpe to rank you on yet |
| **Sharpe** | The ranking figure. Hover a dash to see why it is empty |
| **PnL %** | Your return from trading, with deposits and withdrawals removed |
| **Volume** | Notional of every order filled during the competition. Both sides count: opening a 500 USDC position and closing it is 1,000 |
| **Equity** | Your account value inside the competition window. It freezes when the competition ends |
| **Days** | How many days of returns your score rests on |
| **Trades** | Closed orders during the competition |
| **Spot / Perp** | Your current balance on each. A dash means you did not register that venue |

### Leaderboard tabs
- **Leaderboard:** everyone who has qualified, ranked.
- **Not yet qualified:** everyone who has not, listed with the reason. You are still synced, still scored, and still in the data. Nobody is ever removed from the board; somebody who vanished could not tell that from never having registered.
